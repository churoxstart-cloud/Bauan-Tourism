What the portal does

Everything the tourism office needs, in one place

Built directly from the Department of Tourism's own accommodation and visitor recording formats.

Digital daily logs

Resort staff record guest check-ins, overnight stays and room occupancy, with no more paper DAE-1A forms.

Attraction visitor tracking

Log day-visitor counts per attraction with the same breakdown as the official VAR-1 form.

Consolidated MTO reports

The Municipal Tourism Office sees an always-current annual summary across every resort.

Program announcements

The MTO posts tourism programs and initiatives to every resort's dashboard, or only to selected resorts.

Controlled access

Every account is reviewed and approved by an administrator before it can log in.

Works on any device

Fully responsive, so it is usable from a front-desk computer, a tablet or a phone.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who can register?

Staff of the Municipal Tourism Office and authorized staff of accommodation establishments in Bauan, Batangas.

What happens after I register?

Your account is created as pending. An administrator reviews it, and once it is approved you can log in. Until then, login is not available.

Which forms does the portal cover?

The monthly accommodation recording format (DAE-1A) and the attraction visitor recording format (VAR-1).

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the login page to receive a reset link.

How is my data handled?

Please read the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Notice before creating an account.

Ready to register?

Create your account today. It is reviewed by an administrator, and you can start recording as soon as it is approved.
